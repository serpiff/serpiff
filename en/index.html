System requirements

Required

  • A Mac with Apple silicon (macOS 27.0 or later).
  • git and gh are installed and on your PATH.

Recommended

  • The language servers for the programming languages you use are installed and on your PATH.
  • claude is installed and on your PATH.

Install

Run the following command in a terminal.

gh release download -R serpiff/serpiff -p install.sh -O - | sh

Open the downloaded .dmg and drag serpiff.app to Applications.

Download the dmg

serpiff is not notarized by Apple, so the first launch fails. Follow the steps below. The steps and wording may differ depending on your macOS version.

  1. Close the warning that appears when the launch fails.
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway next to the message saying “serpiff” was blocked to protect your Mac. This button appears for only about an hour after you try to open the app. If you don't see it, try opening the app again.
  3. In the confirmation dialog, click Open Anyway again and enter your administrator password.
